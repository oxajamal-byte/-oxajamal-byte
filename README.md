## About Me

I'm an incoming high school senior in Canada with a strong interest in cybersecurity, software development, and building things that can actually help people.

I started learning cybersecurity because I wanted to understand what really happens behind the internet we use every day. Since then, I have spent years learning through projects, competitions, research, labs, and real world experience.

I have built projects focused on network security, phishing detection, endpoint monitoring, security education, and beginner friendly cybersecurity tools. Some of my work includes Sentinel Security, Argus Mini EDR, PortPrimer, Warden, and other projects I continue to improve.

Outside of building, I have competed in cybersecurity competitions, researched vulnerabilities, worked with real websites, and written articles about common cyber attacks and how people can protect themselves online.

A big part of cybersecurity for me is helping people who may not have a technical background. I have taught online safety to elementary school students, helped people understand common internet risks, supported small businesses with technology and security, and worked on tools made for organizations that do not have their own security teams.

I have also moved between Canada and the United States throughout high school. Changing schools and countries made school difficult at times, but it also pushed me to keep learning on my own and to keep building wherever I was.

Right now, I am focused on becoming much stronger in networking, detection engineering, security research, Python, and defensive cybersecurity. I am also continuing to improve the projects I have already built instead of constantly moving on to something new.

There is still a lot I want to learn, and that is one of the reasons I enjoy cybersecurity so much.

I build, test, compete, teach, write, learn from mistakes, and then come back better.

**I am still getting started.**
