Across those projects I have written **100+ automated tests**, worked with real Sysmon telemetry, network traffic, security alerts, safe scanning, phishing detection, and tools made for small organizations and beginners.

I competed solo in **picoCTF 2026**, finishing **Top 100 in the U.S. division and #530 globally out of 90,000+ teams**, while solving around **65 challenges** across web, forensics, crypto, binary exploitation, blockchain, and general skills.

Cybersecurity also matters to me outside of code. I have taught online safety to **Grades 4 to 6 students**, published cybersecurity articles, helped maintain and monitor real business websites, and worked on making security easier to understand for people who do not have a technical background.

I have moved between **Canada and the United States and attended five schools during high school**, so a lot of what I know came from teaching myself, building things, breaking them, fixing them, and trying again.

Right now I am focused on getting my projects in front of real users, improving my networking and detection skills, studying security deeper, and continuing to build work I can actually be proud of.
